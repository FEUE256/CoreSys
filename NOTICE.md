# NOTICE

## Today's Topic: COP (CoreSys File System Omega Pro)

During today's internal development meeting, the CoreSys team discussed the current state and future direction of COP (CoreSys File System Omega Pro), which is planned to become one of the most important storage components within the CoreSys ecosystem.

After extensive internal evaluation and planning, it has been confirmed that Version 0.1.2.1 will require a significantly longer development cycle compared to previous releases. The reason for this extended timeline is the ongoing implementation and testing of the COP filesystem architecture.

The development team, together with FÈUE leadership, agreed that stability, filesystem integrity, and long-term scalability are more important than rapid release scheduling. Because of this, the next public release may take considerably more time before becoming available.

According to statements made during the meeting, this release represents a transition from early prototype-level infrastructure toward a more serious and modular operating system architecture.

Nils Efverman, CEO of FÈUE, stated that the objective of Version 0.1.2.1 is not simply to introduce another update, but to establish a stronger technical foundation for future CoreSys development
The development team thanks all followers, testers, and contributors for their patience during this longer development period.

Further technical information and development updates will be released in future notices.

— Nils Efverman
CEO, FÈUE
